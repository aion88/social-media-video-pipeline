# Fonts

`InterDisplay-Medium.ttf` and `InterDisplay-Light.ttf` come from the Inter project
(Copyright 2016 The Inter Project Authors), licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org). They are converted from the `.woff2` files shipped in
`.claude/skills/sm-motion/template/assets/fonts/`. Caveat and JetBrains Mono there are OFL too.

To use your own font for the subtitles, set `"polices"` in `overlays.json` (see `overlays.py`).
